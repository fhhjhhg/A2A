## 2024-05-15 - [Parallelizing Notebook Subprocess Pipelines]
**Learning:** When using `subprocess.Popen` to chain commands (like `wget | tar`) in parallel using `ThreadPoolExecutor`, redirecting stdout/stderr to `subprocess.PIPE` without reading from them can fill the OS pipe buffer and cause deadlocks. Also, IPython's `!` magic is not thread-safe and executes sequentially in loops.
**Action:** Use `subprocess.Popen` with `ThreadPoolExecutor`, route unneeded streams to `subprocess.DEVNULL`, explicitly call `.wait()` on the upstream process after `.communicate()` on downstream, and close upstream stdout early (`p1.stdout.close()`) to allow SIGPIPE handling. Always iterate over `as_completed(futures)` with `.result()` rather than `.map()` for responsive UI updates and exception propagation.
