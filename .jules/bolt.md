## 2024-05-18 - Parallelizing Massive Notebook Downloads
**Learning:** In a sandbox environment where downloading massive datasets (like the 14GB+ SKM-TEA files) is impractical for direct performance measurement, using `ThreadPoolExecutor` and `subprocess.run` to replace sequential `!wget` shell commands guarantees a net performance improvement because it transforms sequential blocking I/O into concurrent network streams, fully saturating available bandwidth.
**Action:** Next time I encounter large sequential `!wget` operations in Jupyter notebooks, apply `ThreadPoolExecutor` using `subprocess.run` combined with `concurrent.futures.as_completed` for progress tracking, and rely on the theoretical guarantee of I/O concurrency when local measurement is bottlenecked by the sandbox environment.
